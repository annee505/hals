# Skill Assessment Questions

3.  **Data Structures:** Do you know how to use lists and dictionaries/objects to store structured data? (Yes/No)

## Math & Statistics
4.  **Math Level:** Have you taken any courses in Calculus or Linear Algebra? (Basic/Intermediate/Advanced/None)
5.  **Statistics:** Are you comfortable calculating and interpreting mean, median, and variance? (Yes/No)

## Goals & Motivation
6.  **Time Commitment:** How many hours per week can you consistently dedicate to learning? (5/10/15+)

8.  **Project Preference:** Do you prefer guided tutorials or open-ended projects?

10. **Web Check:** Do you know the difference between HTML, CSS, and JavaScript? (Yes/No)
