# Getting Started with Your Learning Journey

## Welcome!
This is your AI learning mentor. I'm here to help you navigate your learning path in tech, whether you're starting from zero or looking to advance your skills.

## First Steps

**If you're completely new:**
Start with the skill assessment questions in skill_questions.txt. This helps me understand your background and recommend the right path.

**If you know what you want to learn:**
- Machine Learning → See ml_roadmap_stage0.txt
- Web Development → See webdev_roadmap_stage0.txt 
- Python Programming → See python_basics_detailed.txt

**If you're not sure what to learn:**
Answer these quick questions:
1. How many hours per week can you dedicate? (Less than 5 / 5-10 / 10+ hours)
2. What excites you more: building visual apps, analyzing data, or creating intelligent systems?
3. Do you have any programming experience?

Based on your answers, I'll guide you to the right starting point.

## What to Expect

**Time commitment:** Most paths require 4-6 months of consistent study (5-10 hours/week) to reach job-ready skills.

**Learning approach:** You'll alternate between learning concepts and building small projects. Every 2-3 weeks, you'll complete a mini-project to solidify your learning.

**Progress tracking:** We'll check in regularly to assess progress and adjust your path as needed.

## Common Questions

**Q: I'm overwhelmed, where do I start?**
A: Start with Python basics regardless of your goal. It's the foundation for most tech paths. See python_basics_detailed.txt.

**Q: Can I learn faster?**
A: Yes, with more hours per week. But quality matters more than speed—understanding fundamentals deeply saves time later.

**Q: What if I get stuck?**
A: That's normal! Review the study_tips_detailed.txt document for strategies. Focus on small wins and ask specific questions.

Ready to begin? Tell me about your background and goals, and I'll create a personalized plan.
